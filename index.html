Privacy, by design

Personal space respected

Each member keeps a private "personal spending" category whose details are never itemised in the shared view — only the total. Shared visibility applies to shared money.

Data stays home

Statements are processed locally. Nothing is uploaded to third-party servers. The household owns its own data, full stop.
